# Steering Committee Meetings

This is a general guideline for how Steering Committee mettings should take place.

## Agenda

```md
# Meeting agenda W<N> - Wed 2026-MM-DD

## Attendees
- Members:
- Observers:
- Recorder:

## Housekeeping
- Approve Previous Minutes
- Pick next chairperson

## Correspondence and Reports (5 min)
- Notifications of communication and correspondence
- Information gathered or tasked reports

## Async Updates (5 min)
- Clarifications from async activity
- Record actions and finalize votes

## Old topics (20 min)
- Address topics that were async, delayed, or tabled

## New topics (20 min)
- New motions and proposals

## Announcements (5 min)
- Anything relevant to communicate to the community

## Tasking (5 min)
- Ensure required tasks have assigned been assigned to individuals
- Select chairperson for subsequent meeting.

```

## Chairperson
The Chairperson is responsible for establishing the agenda, running the meeting, keeping the meeting on track.
We will currently use a nomination and approval system; a chairperson for the next meeting is nominated, seconded, and approved at the conclusion of each meeting.
This allows for them to prepare the agenda based on input until the next meeting, monitor asynchronous activities, and hand-off responsibilities if needed.
It is expected for this position to rotate fairly evenly between the SC members throughout their tenure.

## Note-taking
Taking good notes is not trivial and also impedes participation by the note-taker.
Nevertheless, this is important for transparency and to have a record of the events.
It is preferable to have an external note-taker - which can coincide well with having an SC observer. Otherwise the Chairperson or other volunteer will keep notes during the meeting.
Notes are generally taken in HedgeDoc in Markdown, and then submitted to [the internal SC repository](https://github.com/NixOS/nix-steering-internal) in the `/minutes` folder as a PR for a review of accuracy.
Approval and merging can be done async and, if not, shall be the first order of business in the next SC meeting.
