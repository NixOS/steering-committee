# Team Appointment and responsibilities

The SC shall steer, not manage:

- [SC constitution docs](https://github.com/NixOS/org/blob/main/doc/constitution.md)
- No responsibility without authority ([see also @tomberek's values thread](https://discourse.nixos.org/t/what-are-the-values-that-you-see-being-expressed-in-the-nixos-project/15959/8))
  - Protection from being overturned
- Precedents for teams asking for self determination
  - @lassulus, carrying moderation at the time, ["Want mods to be required to agree to new mods, don't want SC to be able to appoint mods without mods agreeing."](https://github.com/NixOS/steering-committee/blob/main/minutes/2025-11-05.md)
  - Previous votes ([Vote 0004](https://github.com/NixOS/steering-committee/blob/main/vote-logs/0004-bootstrap-community-team.md)) already formed consensus for self determination: "The SC is
not to intervene into day to day business of moderation, even during the interim period"

The goals of this process document are:

- Empowering teams and thus avoid micromanagement-related frustration and churn
- Relieve the SC from non-SC work

## 1. Appointing a team

When the SC appoints a team (e.g. nixpkgs core, moderatin, etc.) it only defines:

- the roles and goals of that team
- One team leader
- Resource allocation (i.e. money, HW, admin access, etc.)

## 2. Team organisation

The SC does not select team members, does not interview individual members etc.
Such tasks are reserved to the team leader:

- The team leader manages the team on their behalf without SC involvement.
- Interviewing, appointing, removing team members is a team-internal matter.
- Deciding how the team fulfills their externally set roles and goals is also a team-internal matter.
- Communication with the SC goes through the responsible person.

The team may organize themselves however they like (e.g. internal democracy etc.), but "answerability" towards the SC cannot be shared.

## 3. Levers of the SC

Apart from defining a teams roles and goals, the SC can only *appoint* and *remove* the team leader.

### Reasons for removal of a team leader

- The team's goals are not met and the team leader provides no sufficent reasons why
- Questions that belong inside the team keep arriving at the SC because the team could not settle
  them (repeated pattern, not one genuinely hard case)
- The team leader will not escalate when their team is truly stuck.
  Leaving contributors with nowhere to go.
- The team leader ignores an external goal decision rather than answering it.

### Illegitimate reasons for removal

- Generally disagreeing with the SC
- Making an unpopular call within the role and goal definition
