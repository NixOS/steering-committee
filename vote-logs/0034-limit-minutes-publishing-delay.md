# 0034: Limit the delay in publishing meeting minutes

## Context

Publishing of the SC meeting minutes was often delayed for no good reason. This sets a baseline for when minutes can be published, so the next SC doesn't have to reinvent it.

**Proposed By:** Robert Hensing

## Motion
> Considering that our publishing of the minutes is often delayed for no good reason, let us remove any uncertainty about whether they can be published.
>
> - Minutes do not require approval, but instead are expected to be posted after a review period of 4 days.
> - The chairperson is expected to post them on the 5th day.
> - Anyone may publish the minutes after the review period.
