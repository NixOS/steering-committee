# 0036: Affirm the authority of Nixpkgs teams, including the CUDA team

Proposal:

> The SC supports making teams which have authority over areas of Nixpkgs, of which the Nixpkgs core team is the largest scope.
> Those teams have self-determination, and are expected to live up to the Nix community values of respect and civility, growing trust through doing work together, and gaining increasing trust over time.
> We re-affirm that the CUDA team has authority over CUDA in Nixpkgs, and we support the team member's decisions as to their membership.

Voted on during SC + Board meeting on 2026-11-04.
