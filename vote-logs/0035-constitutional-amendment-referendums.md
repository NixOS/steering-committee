# 0035: Constitutional amendment: referendums

## Context

This amendment adds a Referendums subsection to the SC decision process in the [constitution](https://github.com/NixOS/org/blob/main/doc/constitution.md), letting the SC put binding or non-binding votes to the community.

**Proposed By:** Philip Taron

## Motion
> The SC may, by simple majority, put a non-binding vote to the community, and by super-majority, a binding vote.
>
> A binding referendum results in the adoption of some new policy.
> A non-binding referendum is advisory, and functions like a large opinion poll.
>
> Eligibility follows Vote eligibility. The vote is administered by the Election Committee.
>
> The SC must publish the question and the available options at least four weeks before voting opens. Binding referendums must include a threshold required to pass.
> If a binding referendum passes it shall take effect in the following way:
> - During the first year it requires an extraordinary decision of the SC to overturn it
> - After one year, it takes a mere ordinary decision of the SC to overturn it.

Voted on during the [2026-10-21 meeting](../minutes/2026-10-21.md).
