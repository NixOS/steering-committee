# 0027: Appeal policy created by the Bootstrap team

## Context and Subject
A vote on the appeal policy created by the Bootstrap team: https://github.com/NixOS/moderation/pull/44

> The SC being the highest instance in the community currently already organically receives the appeals of the people unhappy about a moderation decision.
> This proposal codifies a process that is already happening.
> Having a process about it gives people transparency on how things are handled.
> It doesn't address the question of re-integration of confirmed bans, only gives people an opportunity to appeal the decision when it is being made.

**Proposed By:** Julien Malka

Opened 2026-08-19 for 5 days.
