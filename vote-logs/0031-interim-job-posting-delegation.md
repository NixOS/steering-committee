# 0031: Interim delegation of job posting decisions to the moderation team

## Context and Subject
Following discussion of military job postings, the Steering Committee proposed delegating decisions on job postings to the moderation team while the SC develops a more formal policy. The motion allows the moderation team to act autonomously while keeping the SC informed, and preserves the SC's ability to weigh in.

A couple of SC members had highlighted that there was likely not yet a clear consensus on the best approach or what a full policy should look like. Reaching that clarity and developing a considered policy would take more time. The interim delegation allows the moderation team to act while the SC works through that discussion, with a clear sunset clause for when a superseding policy is approved.

**Proposed By:** cafkafk

**Opened:** 2026-09-09 at 18:00 CEST (UTC+02:00, Europe/Copenhagen)
**Scheduled Close:** 2026-09-16 at 18:00 CEST (UTC+02:00, Europe/Copenhagen)
**Closed Early:** 2026-09-10 at 07:36:40 CEST (UTC+02:00, Europe/Copenhagen)

## Motion
> Until the steering committee writes a policy on what job postings are allowed, we allow the moderation team to make decisions at their discretion. This wouldn't stop the SC from weighing in on these issues, but the moderation can act autonomously on these decisions, giving the SC a heads up when making them.
> This policy would sunset the moment a policy said to superseed it is voted in favor.
