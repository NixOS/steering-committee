Some talk about whether the SC should vote on @philiptaron's document / proposal from Jun 2025:

> 1. You are responsible for what you merge.
>    - So is the author.
>    - Since you have the power to correct it, you are often **more** responsible.
> 2. You are responsible for how you behave in reviewing code.
>    - You are entrusted with upholding Nixpkgs-wide quality
>    - You are entrusted with making Nixpkgs a welcoming and inclusive space.
>    - This means you cannot make up a standard and personally enforce it.
>    - This means you defer to other reviewers if they understand the area better than you.
> 3. You are responsible for learning.
>    - If you are given feedback by others, take it to heart, or fight it fairly.
>    - If you are given feedback by the committer team in particular, they will note whether your privilege is at stake.
> 4. You are responsible for teaching.
>    - Don't just correct, explain.
>    - Don't just ❌, encourage what to do next.
> 5. Merging your own PRs is acceptable.
>    - Don't do so to avoid community discussion.
>    - Don't do so and break things.
>      Again, you are responsible for what you merge.
> 6. You have limits.
>    Please honor them and allow for other's efforts to come in, without gating whole areas on yourself and your review.
> 7. When you do break things, acknowledge your mistake and apologize.
>    - Try to make it better either by changing your behavior in future or by undoing what has been broken.
