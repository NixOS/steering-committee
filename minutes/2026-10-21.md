## Old topics (20 min)

### Constitutional amendment: referendums

Proposed text, presented by @philiptaron:

> The SC may, by simple majority, put a non-binding vote to the community, and by super-majority, a binding vote.
  >
> A binding referendum results in the adoption of some new policy.
> A non-binding referendum is advisory, and functions like a large opinion poll.
  >
> Eligibility follows Vote eligibility. The vote is administered by the Election Committee.
  >
> The SC must publish the question and the available options at least four weeks before voting opens. Binding referendums must include a threshold required to pass.
> If a binding referendum passes it shall take effect in the following way:
> - During the first year it requires an extraordinary decision of the SC to overturn it
> - After one year, it takes a mere ordinary decision of the SC to overturn it.

(See https://github.com/NixOS/org/blob/main/doc/constitution.md#ordinary-decisions for the definitions of ordinary and extraordinary decisions.)

- @philiptaron: five members present; if everyone here says yes, this becomes part of the constitution, and a single no blocks it
- @cafkafk: good enough; we've discussed this many times, and it meets all the objections I've heard. We can always improve it later
- @JulienMalka: agrees with the text, but proposes opening a PR to NixOS/org and letting it sit for a week or two so the community can react and suggest improvements before merging. The goal is to be more participative, not to block it
- @cafkafk: how long until the new SC? If we wait too long, @philiptaron may not be here for it
  - @JulienMalka: two or three weeks
  - @philiptaron: if it's three weeks, I'm out
  - Agreed on one week
- @Ericson2314: approves; the language explaining what a binding referendum needs is satisfactory
